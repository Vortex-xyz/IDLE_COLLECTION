Replace these notes with real art. The game draws ColorRect-style shapes in BattleView.gd.

Suggested swaps:
- Monster bodies: drop sprites in Assets/monsters/<id>.png and draw them in BattleView._draw_monster.
- Heroes: Assets/enemies/squire.png, runner.png, knight.png, boss.png.
- Stair and vault: a portrait background in Assets/bg_stair.png.
- UI: a Theme resource with Cinzel/Outfit (or any serif + sans) assigned on the root Control.
- SFX: Assets/sfx/*.ogg, played from GameManager where the "SFX:" comments are.
- Icons: res://icon.png (optional). The project runs without one.

Do not rename monster ids. MonsterDatabase and saves key off them.
